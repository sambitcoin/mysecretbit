₿ BITCOIN SEED BACKUP

Self-Custody Resource for Bitcoin Hodlers
Secure your Bitcoin by backing up your seed phrase properly. A seed phrase is your master key. Losing it means permanent loss of funds. This guide covers the essential methods for generating, storing, and backing up your recovery phrase securely.

Seed Formats: BIP39, SLIP-39 & Multisig

Understand the three main approaches to seed backup and recovery.

Feature BIP39 SLIP-39 Multisig
Structure Single 12-24 word phrase Multiple shares (2-of-3, 3-of-5) Multiple independent seeds
Single Point of Failure Yes - one loss = no recovery No - requires N shares to recover No - requires M of N keys
Theft Risk One backup stolen = all funds at risk Attacker needs multiple shares Attacker needs multiple keys
Wallet Support Universal - all wallets Limited support Most hardware wallets
Best For Standard backups, most users High-security setups Large holdings, institutions

Backup Methods

Metal Seed Plates with Stamping Kit

Durable steel plates that survive fire, water, and corrosion. Stamp your seed phrase manually with letter punches.

  • Steel/titanium construction
  • Survives 1000°C+ fires
  • Complete offline process
→ Metal Storage Comparison

Paper Backup

Print your seed phrase on paper. Simple but vulnerable to fire and water—use as temporary reference only, not long-term storage.

  • Low cost to create
  • Quick to generate
  • Poor durability for long-term
→ bitaddress.org

Steganographic Backup

Hide your recovery instructions in photos using LSB steganography. Dual-layer security: hidden + obfuscated.

  • Compression-resistant encoding
  • Plausible deniability
  • 9-bit redundant backup
→ My Secret Bit

Backup Best Practices

✓ Multi-Backup Strategy:
  • Generate seed offline using verified tools
  • Create multiple physical backups (metal + paper + steganography)
  • Store backups in geographically diverse locations
  • Keep seed and passphrase completely separate
  • Never store seed digitally or on internet-connected devices
  • Test recovery before funding your wallet
  • Use redundancy—no single backup method is perfect
