Understanding File Steganography

My Secret Bit uses Least Significant Bit (LSB) steganography to hide data inside files. By modifying the least significant bits of file data, you can hide messages without perceptibly changing the original file. This works with any digital file—images, audio, video, or binary data.

Email-Safe Carrier Files (Most to Least Common)

📄 PDF

Most common, email-safe. Rarely compressed. Excellent for recovery instructions.

📝 Word (DOCX/DOC)

Very common, email-safe. Preserved in email transmission. Good capacity.

📊 Excel (XLSX)

Spreadsheets, email-safe. Lossless format. Uncompressed by email providers.

🎬 PowerPoint (PPTX)

Presentations, email-safe. Binary container format, preserved in email.

📦 ZIP/Archive

Binary container. Email-safe. Can package multiple files for distribution.

📄 Text (TXT)

Plain text. Always email-safe. Limited capacity but simple and reliable.

⚠️ Why Image & Audio Formats Are Not Supported

Email providers (Gmail, Outlook, etc.) automatically compress and re-encode images and audio. This destroys LSB-hidden data. PNG, JPEG, WAV, MP3, GIF, and similar formats cannot reliably survive email transmission with hidden data intact.

For Bitcoin Self-Custody

Strategy: Hide encrypted recovery instructions in a family photo. The photo looks normal, but contains vague clues only you understand: "Grandma's safe, behind painting" or "Dad's office, third drawer left side."

Multi-layer approach:

  • Hide encrypted instructions in photos/audio (steganography)
  • Store encrypted backup in metal plate or paper in physical location
  • Keep passphrase separate from seed backup
  • Share clues with trusted family member via different channel
  • Test recovery before trusting with real funds
⚠️ Important Limitations

This tool uses basic LSB encoding. For critical Bitcoin backups, consider:

  • Always encrypt data before hiding (AES-256 minimum)
  • Never re-compress files after encoding (destroys hidden data)
  • Test recovery before storing critical data
  • Use redundancy: multiple copies in different formats/locations
  • This is ONE layer of security—combine with other methods
